What we believe

Our Charter

What the Scripture says about the word of God, and who he is.

The King James Bible is the infallible word of God

We hold the King James (Authorized) Version to be the infallible, preserved word of God in English — given by inspiration, pure in every word, and standing for ever.

“All scripture [is] given by inspiration of God, and [is] profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness.”

2 Timothy 3:16

“The words of the LORD [are] pure words: [as] silver tried in a furnace of earth, purified seven times.”

Psalm 12:6

“Thou shalt keep them, O LORD, thou shalt preserve them from this generation for ever.”

Psalm 12:7

“Heaven and earth shall pass away, but my words shall not pass away.”

Matthew 24:35

“Every word of God [is] pure: he [is] a shield unto them that put their trust in him.”

Proverbs 30:5

“Thy word [is] true from the beginning: and every one of thy righteous judgments [endureth] for ever.”

Psalm 119:160

“The grass withereth, the flower fadeth: but the word of our God shall stand for ever.”

Isaiah 40:8

There is but one Lawgiver, and his law stands

Every throne, and every law made by men, answers to a single authority. He who created all things alone has the right to make law, and having made it, he alone upholds it. There is no second lawgiver — not in heaven, and not upon any throne among men. And his law is not an idea, nor a counsel, nor one rule among many: it is his own word, given by inspiration, unalterable, and the standard by which every other claim to authority is judged.

“For the LORD [is] our judge, the LORD [is] our lawgiver, the LORD [is] our king; he will save us.”

Isaiah 33:22

“Ye shall not add unto the word which I command you, neither shall ye diminish [ought] from it, that ye may keep the commandments of the LORD your God which I command you.”

Deuteronomy 4:2

“For verily I say unto you, Till heaven and earth pass, one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law, till all be fulfilled.”

Matthew 5:18

“Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets: I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil.”

Matthew 5:17

Jesus is King

The one fold has one shepherd, and he is King of kings and Lord of lords. His kingdom is everlasting, and every knee shall bow to him.

“The Lord God shall give unto him the throne of his father David: And he shall reign over the house of Jacob for ever; and of his kingdom there shall be no end.”

Luke 1:32–33

“That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow… and [that] every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ [is] Lord, to the glory of God the Father.”

Philippians 2:10–11

“And he hath on [his] vesture and on his thigh a name written, KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS.”

Revelation 19:16

The signatures in the text

Counted through the whole text, phrase after phrase falls on an exact multiple of seven, and the totals answer to one another the way a seal answers to its impression. Hold fast

These things we hold, and hold fast

None of this is new, and none of it is our own invention. It is the faith once delivered to the saints. We neither add to the word of God nor take from it; our part is to keep it — to hold fast the form of sound words in faith and love, and to contend earnestly for the truth that was given, not devised.

“Hold fast the form of sound words, which thou hast heard of me, in faith and love which is in Christ Jesus.”

2 Timothy 1:13

“Beloved, when I gave all diligence to write unto you of the common salvation, it was needful for me to write unto you, and exhort [you] that ye should earnestly contend for the faith which was once delivered unto the saints.”

Jude 1:3

Questions on Our Charter

Why do you quote only the King James Bible?

Because we hold it to be the infallible, preserved word of God in English — given by inspiration, pure in every word, and standing for ever (2 Timothy 3:16; Psalm 12:6–7; Matthew 24:35). It is our standard for doctrine and practice.

What authority does the King James Bible have?

Full authority. It is the word of the one Lawgiver, given by inspiration, and it stands over every doctrine, every council, and every claim of men: “All scripture [is] given by inspiration of God, and [is] profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness” (2 Timothy 3:16). Nothing may be added to it, neither diminished from it (Deuteronomy 4:2), and “heaven and earth shall pass away, but my words shall not pass away” (Matthew 24:35). By it every question of doctrine and practice is settled.

Are we saved by good works?

No. Only by Jesus the King. “For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: not of works, lest any man should boast.” (Ephesians 2:8–9) And “neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved” (Acts 4:12) — no work, no rite, and no membership of men. Good works are the evidence of belonging, not the price of it.

Are we under the law, or under grace alone?

Grace saves us; the law still governs us. Paul says we are “not under the law, but under grace” (Romans 6:14) — meaning that no one can earn salvation by keeping it. But the one who keeps it is the one the Lawgiver sent, and he said “Think not that I am come to destroy the law… I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil” (Matthew 5:17). His law is the royal law, and it stands: “If ye fulfil the royal law according to the scripture, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself, ye do well” (James 2:8).
