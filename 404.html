Lost? A few quick answers

Where should I go from here?

Start at the home page, or use the menu above to reach The Charter, Citizenship, or The King.

Did the page move or get renamed?

It may have. Some pages have new names — the page once called “Beliefs” is now The Charter, “Welcome” is now Citizenship, and “About” is now Heritage, and “Contact” is now Benefits.

Can I still reach you?

Yes — you can always apply here.

Where should a newcomer begin?

Try the Charter for what we believe, or Citizenship for how one enters the kingdom. The King page tells of him who reigns over all.

Is there a word for a seeker?

Yes. “Seek ye the LORD while he may be found, call ye upon him while he is near.” (Isaiah 55:6, KJB) “And ye shall seek me, and find [me], when ye shall search for me with all your heart.” (Jeremiah 29:13, KJB)
