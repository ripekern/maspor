A word for the way

A wrong turn is not the end of the road. The ones who seek him will find him, and the one that was lost is worth leaving ninety-nine for. Nothing on this site is beyond reach, and no one who arrives here at a dead end has been written off.

A few quick answers

Where should I go from here?

Start at the home page, or use the menu above. If you are new to us, read Our Charter for what we believe and Our Heritage for what marks his people — the second of those is the page we would ask you to read twice.

Did the page move or get renamed?

It may have. Some pages have new names — the page once called “Beliefs” is now Our Charter, “Welcome” is now Our Citizens, “About” is now Our Heritage, and “Contact” is now Our Benefits.

Can I still reach you?

Yes — you can always write to us, by email or by post. A broken link is our fault, and we would rather hear about it.

Is there a word for a seeker?

Yes. “Seek ye the LORD while he may be found, call ye upon him while he is near.” (Isaiah 55:6) “And ye shall seek me, and find [me], when ye shall search for me with all your heart.” (Jeremiah 29:13)
