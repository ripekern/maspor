## Remaining artwork

`images/maspor-seal.png` is a coat of arms bearing the words **REPUBLIKA MANORSKA**.
It is *not* the arms of Maspor. It was carried over from earlier material and does
not match the site it appears on. The file is named for the site, but the alt text
describes what the image actually shows, so screen readers are not misled. It should
be replaced with genuine arms before this site represents itself as a government.

## Bitcoin Cash badges

`images/bitcoin-cash-badge.svg` is the only variant kept. The issuer's `-gold` and
`-framed` colourways were never used on the site and have been deleted rather than
carried as unused weight.

## Derived files

Every photograph above is also generated at two widths in AVIF and WebP
(`name-480.*`, `name-960.*`), referenced from `<picture>` with the original JPEG as
the fallback. The originals are kept at full size and remain the correct file to
edit: re-derive from them rather than from a derivative. All three formats are
re-encoded without an ICC profile and, for JPEG, progressively.

## Removed

Three images were deleted from this directory as part of the October 2026 tidy-up
because they were third-party works still under copyright, and redistributing them
on a public site was not defensible:

- a hand-lettered fruit print signed by its illustrator
- a painting of Christ on a white horse, credited in its own border to a copyright holder
- watermarked stock clipart

Their four replacements are the CC0 works listed above.
