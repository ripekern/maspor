Heirs of the promise

Our heritage in Christ

The family of God has an inheritance — that is a promise. All who are of faith are Abraham's seed, heirs according to the promise. They are known by their fruits, by the conduct of their private lives, and by gifts that are given of the Spirit.

Heirs by promise and faith

Our heritage is not restricted by borders. It is given by promise and received by faith — to everyone who believes that Jesus Christ is Lord of all.

“Know ye therefore that they which are of faith, the same are the children of Abraham.”

Galatians 3:7

“And if ye [be] Christ's, then are ye Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise.”

Galatians 3:29

“They which are the children of the flesh, these [are] not the children of God: but the children of the promise are counted for the seed.”

Romans 9:8

The promise is borderless and not defined by the world.

An inheritance reserved in heaven

The inheritance of God's children cannot be lost, defiled, or taken away. It is kept for them, and they are kept for it.

“To an inheritance incorruptible, and undefiled, and that fadeth not away, reserved in heaven for you.”

1 Peter 1:4

“And if children, then heirs; heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ.”

Romans 8:17

“Wherefore thou art no more a servant, but a son; and if a son, then an heir of God through Christ.”

Galatians 4:7

“Giving thanks unto the Father, which hath made us meet to be partakers of the inheritance of the saints in light.”

Colossians 1:12

How they conduct their private lives

God's people are known by their fruits — not by the office they hold, but by what they are when no man is watching. The grace of God compels them to deny ungodliness and to live soberly, righteously, and godly in this present world: the private conduct carries the name Christian.

“Wherefore by their fruits ye shall know them.”

Matthew 7:20

“Teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should live soberly, righteously, and godly, in this present world;”

Titus 2:12

“Finally, brethren, whatsoever things are true, whatsoever things [are] honest, whatsoever things [are] just, whatsoever things [are] pure, whatsoever things [are] lovely, whatsoever things [are] of good report; if [there] [be] any virtue, and if [there] [be] any praise, think on these things.”

Philippians 4:8

“But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, [and] easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy.”

James 3:17

Their gifts

God's people also carry gifts — not offices of government, but endowments of the Spirit given to each one for the good of all. The word of wisdom, the word of knowledge, faith, healing, prophecy, discerning of spirits: no man takes these to himself; he receives them, and ministers the same to another.

“For to one is given by the Spirit the word of wisdom; to another the word of knowledge by the same Spirit; To another faith by the same Spirit; to another the gifts of healing by the same Spirit; To another the working of miracles; to another prophecy; to another discerning of spirits; to another [divers] kinds of tongues; to another the interpretation of tongues:”

1 Corinthians 12:8–10

“As every man hath received the gift, [even] [so] minister the same one to another, as good stewards of the manifold grace of God.”

1 Peter 4:10

The promise is to you and your children

The covenant given to Abraham was never fenced to one family of flesh. God promised that in him all the families of the earth would be blessed — a promise reaching to all who are afar off, as many as the Lord shall call.

“And I will bless them that bless thee… and in thee shall all families of the earth be blessed.”

Genesis 12:3

“And I will establish my covenant between me and thee and thy seed after thee in their generations for an everlasting covenant, to be a God unto thee, and to thy seed after thee.”

Genesis 17:7

“For the promise is unto you, and to your children, and to all that are afar off, [even] as many as the Lord our God shall call.”

Acts 2:39

The LORD himself is our portion

The greatest inheritance of God's family is not land or treasure, but God himself. He is the portion of his people, and his compassions fail not.

“The LORD [is] the portion of mine inheritance and of my cup: thou maintainest my lot.”

Psalm 16:5

“The lines are fallen unto me in pleasant [places]; yea, I have a goodly heritage.”

Psalm 16:6

“[It] [is] [of] the LORD'S mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. [They are] new every morning: great [is] thy faithfulness.”

Lamentations 3:22–23

Questions on Our Heritage

Who are the children of Abraham?

All who are of faith. “Know ye therefore that they which are of faith, the same are the children of Abraham.” (Galatians 3:7) The children of the promise are counted for the seed (Romans 9:8).

Is this a group for one people or nation only?

No. God hath made of one blood all nations of men (Acts 17:26), and gathers one fold out of every nation (John 10:16). There is no preferred people and no excluded people in Christ.

What is the inheritance of God's children?

An inheritance incorruptible and undefiled, reserved in heaven (1 Peter 1:4). The children of God are heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ (Romans 8:17).

Do good works earn the inheritance?

No. Belonging is by promise and faith; fruit grows from the root and does not earn the place. The promise is for everyone who believes.
